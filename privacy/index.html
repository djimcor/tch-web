Privacy notice

How Tech Coaching Hub Ltd, including TCH Solutions and TCH Community, uses and protects your personal information.

Last updated: November 2026

1. Who we are

This website is run by Tech Coaching Hub Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales (company no. 17132813), registered office Colchester, UK. TCH Solutions and TCH Community are trading names of Tech Coaching Hub Ltd.

We are the data controller for the personal information described in this notice. ICO registration number: ZC300785.

Questions about your data? Email info@techcoachinghub.com.

2. What we collect

  • When you contact us: your name, email address, business name, website or social media links, and anything you include in your message. Our contact form does not send or store anything itself: it opens your own email app, and we receive the email you choose to send.
  • When we work together: the details we need to deliver the service, send quotes and invoices, and keep business records.
  • When you buy a ready-made kit on Etsy: Etsy shares the order details we need to fulfil your order. Etsy’s own privacy policy covers your purchase.
  • When you visit the website: our hosting provider automatically processes technical data, such as your IP address and browser type, to deliver the site securely.

3. Why we use it and our lawful basis

PurposeLawful basis (UK GDPR)
Replying to your enquiry and preparing your free Online ReportLegitimate interests, or steps before entering into a contract
Delivering services and kits you have orderedContract
Invoicing, accounting and tax recordsLegal obligation

We do not sell your data, and we do not send marketing emails unless you have asked to receive them.

4. Who we share it with

We only share data with trusted providers who help us run the business:

  • Google Workspace, for our business email and files.
  • Google Fonts, which supplies the fonts on this website, so your browser connects to Google’s servers.
  • Etsy, if you buy a kit through our shop.
  • Our accountant and HMRC, where needed for tax and legal reasons.

Some of these providers may process data outside the UK. Where they do, they use legal safeguards approved under UK data protection law.

5. Cookies

We do not use analytics, tracking or advertising cookies. Our hosting provider may set strictly necessary security cookies to protect the website from misuse.

6. How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that don’t lead to work: up to 12 months, then deleted.
  • Client and financial records: 6 years after the end of the financial year they relate to, as UK law requires.

7. Working with your clients’ data

Some services, such as the client data tidy-up, involve information about your own clients. In those cases you remain the data controller and we act on your instructions. We agree how that data is handled before any work starts.

8. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of your data;
  • ask us to correct or delete it;
  • object to or restrict how we use it;
  • ask us to transfer it to you or another organisation.

To use any of these rights, email info@techcoachinghub.com. We will reply within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, please tell us first so we can put it right. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or on 0303 123 1113.

9. TCH Community

If you join TCH Community through Instagram, WhatsApp or Facebook, those platforms’ own privacy policies also apply. We only use the details you share there to run the community.

10. Changes to this notice

We may update this notice from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
