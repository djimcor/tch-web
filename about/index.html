About us

Technology should open doors for everyone.

Tech Coaching Hub helps small businesses work smarter and helps people build careers in tech, through two brands with one shared purpose.

We see the same challenges again and again, and we believe they can be solved with the right tools and support.

Small businesses buried in admin

Owners spend their evenings on paperwork and repetitive tasks instead of on their clients.

Great businesses that can’t be found

Today, most customers search online first. If they can’t find you, they find someone else.

Graduates, and people who never had the chance to study, find it hard to land their first job in tech.

Our vision
A world where socioeconomic status doesn’t define opportunity, but inspires people to reach their full potential.
Our mission
